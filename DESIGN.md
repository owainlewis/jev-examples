# Support desk

Mode: operate. A local inbox used in a Python tutorial and screen recording.

Considered structures: queue table, kanban board, conversation timeline, ticket ledger, split inbox, command palette, and intake wizard. The direction seed assigned candidate five: a split inbox. It keeps the original message beside its classification so viewers can compare evidence and judgment without changing pages.

Use a warm white reading surface, dark ink, thin neutral dividers, and a dark green action color. Native sans-serif body text keeps the tutorial interface familiar. Left navigation filters teams and review status; the main area lists tickets or shows a ticket and its decision details. On narrow screens, navigation wraps above the content. No decorative charts, stock imagery, or fabricated activity.

Ticket text leads. Probability bars are labeled with exact numeric values. Review status and severity have separate labels. Errors say whether the ticket was saved and offer retry. Human corrections remain separate from the original model answer.
