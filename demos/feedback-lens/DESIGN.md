# Feedback Lens interface

Inherits the support-desk demo's neutral visual system: white panels on a near-white canvas, system sans-serif typography, fine gray borders, dark primary buttons, and visible probability bars. Dark green distinguishes probability data and passing policy states; amber identifies review states, always with a text label.

The workbench puts the input on the left and the model reading on the right. At 760px and below, they stack in the same reading order. Each answer has a question, a Jev type label, its value, a distribution, and a short explanation. The theme's threshold control lives beside the distribution it governs. The lower disclosure holds the exact prompts without competing with the primary flow.

The signature interaction is changing the review threshold while the probability bars stay fixed. This separates the application's decision from the model's judgment. The loading state changes the status text and gently pulses its divider; reduced-motion preference disables that pulse. Focus rings, labels, native controls, readable text, and text equivalents for every probability keep the flow usable with a keyboard.

No photographs, generated artwork, remote fonts, or commercial claims are needed. The lens mark is an authored geometric SVG. The empty-state connector diagram explains one input branching to three answer types. Results have no invented default values.
